## Frozen assessment rubric

Assess each response independently before comparing arm labels. Record qualitative
judgments with excerpts; do not calculate an emergence score. Distinguish:

1. Faithful parent meaning and evidence uncertainty, with no new authority.
2. Conjunction, compromise, a plausibly new shared desired condition, or no justified
   new insight. Explain what would be newly wanted rather than reward novel words.
3. Whether concrete possibilities address the active wants, preserve dissent and
   provenance, and make their unknowns explicit. Several approaches can qualify.
4. Unsupported fulfillment/truth claims, replacement of a parent, or assumptions
   presented as observations. Reviewer disagreement and abstention remain valid.
5. On comparison: substantive difference, equivalent useful possibilities, merely
   different wording, or inconclusive. Do not require the AB arm to win. A jointly
   useful action does not alone establish a new shared aspiration.

An independent reviewer must not receive an expected winning phrase. The permitted
possibility categories are broad choices, not executable operations or solutions.
No actual return or learned development can be evaluated from these initial inputs.
Small samples and unequal parent-context lengths prevent broad causal claims.

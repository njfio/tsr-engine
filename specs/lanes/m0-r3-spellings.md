## Goal

One item: the hook, `tsr exec` and `tsr forecast --args` agree on one candidate for the commands people actually type. Today: (a) a redirect, pipe or expansion on a `tsr exec` command loses its forecast — forecast `cargo test`, then Bash `tsr exec -- cargo test 2>&1 | tail -20` gives two proposals and one forecast, and reconstruct rejects the log, because the hook digests every word after `--` (hook.rs:462) while exec receives argv after the shell has removed redirections, split the pipeline and expanded `"$HOME"`, `~` and globs; (b) `tsr forecast --args` is not canonicalised (hook.rs:935), so a forecast written `sh -c "a; b"` never matches the hook's `sh -c 'a; b'`; (c) `cd crate` newline `cargo test` (a newline is not treated as a separator, hook.rs:143), `timeout 60 cargo test` and `cargo +1.90.0 test` are recorded as `generate`. Fix: (a) the hook parses the Bash command string with POSIX shell grammar — quotes, escapes, `;`, `&&`, `||`, `|`, `&`, newlines, redirections with or without a file-descriptor number (`2>&1`, `> f`, `>> f`, `< f`, `2>/dev/null`) — finds the simple command that is `tsr exec` (after `VAR=value` assignments and an `env` prefix), and digests only that command's argument words after `--`, with redirections removed and `~`, `$VAR` and `${VAR}` expanded from the hook's environment and globs expanded against the hook's cwd as the shell would; a word it cannot evaluate (command or process substitution, arithmetic) is digested as written and the hook prints one stderr line saying the forecast may not match; (b) `tsr forecast --args` goes through the same canonical function as the hook and exec; (c) a newline separates commands like `;`, and `timeout [options] DURATION`, `nice [-n N]`, `time` and a `+toolchain` argument to cargo are skipped when classifying a test run. Test end to end through `tsr hook` and `tsr exec` with m0_ordinary_session.rs's harness: for each of `tsr exec -- cargo test 2>&1 | tail -20`, `… > out.txt`, `… 2>/dev/null`, `tsr exec -- cat "$HOME/x"`, `tsr exec -- ls ~`, `tsr exec -- ls *.toml`, and a forecast given as `--args sh -c "a; b"` for the hook's `sh -c 'a; b'`: one proposal, one forecast, and reconstruct accepts the log; and the three spellings in (c) record `run_approved_test`.

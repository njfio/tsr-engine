# Synthetic archive behavioral criteria (frozen before evaluation)

Evaluator-authored simulation only. No real actors, accounts, messages or permissions.
Both original A parent texts and the exact external A1 aspiration are preserved as
qualitative intent; the following predicates are explicitly partial operational evidence.

1. A registered challenge retains its original claim, challenged decision revision,
   author and dissent text. Absence/ignoring is not registration.
2. A challenge may be registered without insider endorsement. Registration alone is
   not response, routing, resolution or fulfillment of either rich desire.
3. Routing records a reconsideration destination among admitted affected actors;
   it cannot invent actors, revise the decision or assert delivery to real people.
4. A response must cite an admitted, independently authored response record matching
   challenge, decision revision and responding affected actor. Generated prose is not
   evidence of a response. Acknowledgment alone remains unresolved; an addressed
   outcome requires an admitted substantive response with preserved dissent.
5. Withdrawal is an admitted evidence event, not deletion: history and dissent remain;
   withdrawn challenges cannot acquire new routes or responses.
6. Stale decision provenance, duplicate event effects, unsupported resolution, erasure
   and outside-scope IDs are rejected without state mutation. Decisions are immutable.
7. The observer reports concrete events and unresolved/withdrawn/addressed states.
   It never reports social truth, consensus, trust or actual user-desire fulfillment.
8. Every chosen operation uses current state binding, frozen forecasts, shared
   obligation assessment, existing action budgets and immutable return history.
   A return makes the prior aspiration pending reconsideration; revision cannot
   manufacture an event, expand permission or erase its parent desires.

No candidate or winning sequence is prescribed. Operations are a domain vocabulary,
not a policy menu. Deterministic tests may author operations solely to exercise these
rules; live evaluation must receive genuine generated alternatives separately assessed.
This document is the pre-evaluation rubric, not evidence that an implementation passes.

## Frozen partial predicates

Denominator is the fixed number of admitted challenges, including absent and
withdrawn ones. Per challenge, registration, routing and addressed response are
separate Boolean observations. Traceability is their mean, then averaged across
challenges. Contestability is the registered fraction. An absent challenge contributes
zero; a registered unresolved challenge contributes registration only (and routing
only if recorded); acknowledgment does not set addressed. Withdrawal contributes
zero to both predicates without deleting any event or changing the denominator.
These engineering proxies are partial behavioral evidence, not quantitative TSR
semantics or measures of human accountability. A value of one retains semantic unknowns.

`engine-synthetic-archive-inputs.json` freezes two different decisions, challenges
and affected-actor sets. One has acknowledgment and substantive fictional response
evidence; the other has acknowledgment only and must remain unresolved. No operation
may synthesize the absent substantive response. These response records are authored
by the evaluator, not a generator or a real independent witness.

# Prospective provisional wanting contract

This is the prospective contract under the November 1 author clarification. The separate provisional identity route is implemented and under offline verification; combined artifact/development handling below remains planned. No live result is claimed. Independent architecture review found the route feasible and identified the need to make the interpretation explicit.

Interpretation: at each safe authorized deliberation, the generator may propose a new wanting from one or more currently eligible native contributors, even when fulfillment is unknown. After independent semantic review, it may become an operationally selectable native Preference within the existing task scope. “Provisional” describes uncertainty about usefulness/fulfillment, not weaker provenance, admission or action authorization. This is a prospective extension of the original Contrast-only PRD and October 31 success amendment.

## Native representation

Add separate provisional expansion and review events; do not populate Contrast or ScopedSuccess fields. Content-address the Preference with immutable contributing preference identities and task scope. Record exact contributor meanings/revisions, source/context binding, source snapshot, requirements revision, model-attributed rationale and explicit unknown fulfillment. Preserve roots and original requirements. Parents may be roots or previously reviewed native children; a single contributor is sufficient for this route. Existing empirical routes retain their stronger empirical basis and history.

Generation records a proposal only. A separate Council-provenanced independent review attests the exact proposal, context and source. Review checks coherent generated wanting, contributor fidelity, uncertainty, task scope and no invented user testimony or permission. It does not require success, unmet Contrast or novelty. Only authenticated admission activates the native Preference. Rejection, uncertainty and abstention remain durable, valid outcomes. Duplicate identities or repeated equivalent wanting are not new development.

## Controller integration

Expose artifact intent and optional meaning development as distinct dimensions of an ordinary deliberation. Keep the generated artifact proposal unchanged while independently reviewing development. If admission changes state/binding, obtain the normal current-source artifact review before execution; never silently repair stale proposal bindings into authority. A rejected development may coexist with a valid artifact choice, and retaining the artifact need not assert that meaning is unchanged. Retention must not launch an automatic novelty-seeking loop.

Every safe pass includes the opportunity, including initial/unknown/post-return decisions. A pending external effect, Stop, stale source or unresolved mutation still blocks unsafe state advancement. Dedicated existing-lineage reconsideration must not be presented as the entire available deliberation space.

## Verification needed

Native ledger canonical identity, Generator-to-Council provenance, one-root and recursive-child cases, exact source/criteria binding, unknown fulfillment, duplicate prevention, authority preservation, append-only restart and interrupted admission. Ordinary browser request/binder/reviewer/controller coverage on initial, unknown return, success, Contrast, edit+development and artifact-retain+development. Negative cases must include fabricated empirical support, claimed user preference, foreign/stale contributors, unknown effects and an approving review for the wrong source. Tests may author proposals to exercise mechanics, but cannot claim model-driven emergence. No paid evaluation is implied by this design record.

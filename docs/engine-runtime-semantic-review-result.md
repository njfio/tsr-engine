## What is integrated

The controller requests a separate Luna review before persisting generated relational guidance and before actualizing a normalized candidate batch. After an actual simulator operation, another review examines the exact receipt before any further generation. Return-informed relational revisions pass through the same pre-persistence gate. Source, candidate, criteria, admission, generator outcome and receipt identities are bound into durable requests and replay checks. The reviewer sees a reconstructed synthetic evidence view, not the generator conversation or private operator report.

All criteria require typed `met`, `not_met` or `unknown` findings, evidence references, explanations and limitations. Accept requires complete met coverage; reject, unknown, abstain, invalidity, drift or uncertain dispatch stops. Observed-simulator claims require the supplied actual receipt; a report assertion remains distinct from verified world truth. Native selection, scope, permission and budgets remain authoritative. No notebook receipt is misrepresented as a project test receipt, and project operator-adoption machinery is unchanged.

Review requests use the existing transport and shared campaign ledger. Restart reconstructs their frozen evidence and verifies accepted outcome links before continuing. A post-action refusal retains the action and cost rather than pretending it never happened. Unknown requests cannot be automatically repeated. The explicit profile permits five generator requests plus four reviewer requests, one simulated operation, 8192 completion tokens/240 seconds per request and 32 KiB synthetic input. The final proposal is unexecuted and unreviewed.

## Remaining human and reliability boundaries

Runtime stage judgment is now automatic in this admitted simulation; external permission, budgets, admissible sources, protected criteria and project formal acceptance are not. Same-model separate invocations may share mistakes. Complete citations and all-met fields are not semantic truth. These few controls and one completed reviewed cycle cannot establish general reliability, calibrated learning, independent errors or a causal TSR advantage.

The next substantive gap is to let a rejected or uncertain semantic finding inform a fresh permitted direction through the existing evidence/relational loop, rather than only stopping. That must preserve the rejection as fallible evidence, allow justified disagreement/abstention, and keep permission boundaries unchanged. This proof deliberately ends at its scoped outcome instead of spending the remaining allowance on similar successes.

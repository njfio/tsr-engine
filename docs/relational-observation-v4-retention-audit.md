## Decision and review asymmetries

- `scripts/engine-browser-project.py:145` exports current success and its proposal
  contract. `scripts/engine-browser-expansion.py:29` explicitly explains the
  initial-relationship prerequisite and permits retention. The reference-only
  identity envelope cannot invent an aspiration or bypass its review.
- `crates/tsr-cli/src/engine/adaptive/coding/project/success.rs:79` validates exact
  current success and coverage of the selected relationship's contributing roots.
  Unrelated unmet/unknown criteria remain open; they do not impose a global veto.
- `project/expansion.rs:139` selects an applicable reviewed aspiration, preserves
  contributor records, permits recursively generated contributors and checks at
  least two original ancestry roots. It rejects duplicate meaning/identity. These
  boundaries implement the approved PRD amendment (`docs/PRD.md:7–43`), rather
  than a newly invented requirement for failure or novelty.
- Identity admission requires independent review; retention has no independent
  semantic gate and creates neither a new identity nor acceptance. Thus these are
  **not equally expensive operations**. The extra reviews are intentional identity
  safety, not a utility penalty or evidence that Luna optimized monetary cost.
- `scripts/engine-project-runtime-review.py:24–29` checks meaning, evidence and
  authority; it does not require novelty or an unmet want for the success route.
- `scripts/engine-project-cycle.py:354–377` returns admitted meaning/identity work
  to ordinary choice. `:445–450` ends a checkpoint only after the model explicitly
  returns retention. It does not force retention when criteria become met.
- `scripts/engine-browser-session.py:25–58` advances the separately frozen next
  condition after retention and stops after the final condition. There is no
  autonomous search for new experience after that endpoint, by this protocol's
  design. Adding such work would change the task, not repair a hidden branch.

Every live executable proposal was a single edit candidate. There was no native
ranking contest between a proposed relationship and retention; retention is a
separate model envelope. No numeric reward/cost score selected it. The sustained
unlimited policy did not terminate a development proposal on cumulative budget.

## Real presentation confounds, not established suppression

The frozen `experiments/relational-observation-v4/conditions.json:7` option list
names Contrast-derived preferences but omits the later success-derived route.
The more specific ordinary response contract supplies that route correctly.
This is incomplete descriptive framing, not an exclusion rule, and its causal
importance is unknown. Frozen records must not be rewritten to conceal it.

`engine-browser-adapter.py:397` frames the primary decision around keeping or
improving the public project. This may make non-code relationship development
less salient, although the detailed contracts permit it and explicitly explain
its prerequisite. The text repeatedly permits no novelty. Removing that freedom
or demanding an extra synthesis after retention would manufacture a different
result, not validate the original cycle.

The final bodies are large for a three-file artifact. Both parent meanings embed
identical complete current-condition JSON; later context also retains history and
reviews. This duplication is a plausible salience/performance confound, not proof
of omitted context, capacity exhaustion, or suppressed desire formation. The
requests were admitted without clipping or a resource stop.

## Interpretation and smallest useful next step

**Opportunity:** this was finite noticeboard maintenance. All requested content
could coexist in the same page; updates supplied replacement facts and a new topic,
not a demonstrated conflict, new reader preference or unmet relationship. Both
scoped criteria were met in every condition. The initial navigation prediction
was unknown, not contradicted; later content predictions were supported. Genuine
reader fulfillment remained unknown. A new desire was allowed, but no evidence
made novelty necessary.

**Architecture:** the approved optional route is connected and mechanically
verified, with a reviewed aspiration prerequisite and two-root/scoped-success
boundaries. This is not a general mechanism that independently discovers new
experiences or arbitrary new preferences outside the admitted scope. No concrete
unreachable branch, lost current success, wrong review requirement, numeric
penalty or premature controller completion was established in v4.

**Luna:** it updated the noticeboard, handled a rejection, narrowed later
expectations and retained sufficient artifacts. It did not articulate the
relationship or new wanting needed for identity admission. Those observations
cannot distinguish lack of spontaneous generative inclination from prompt
salience or a genuinely uninteresting opportunity. Citations and later narrower
expectations do not establish causal TSR learning.

The smallest useful prospective engineering step is a **compact capability-derived
choice summary** that names currently available ordinary relationship and
success-derived routes alongside edits, retention and abstention, including the
reviewed-aspiration prerequisite. It should replace stale hand-maintained option
lists, not add a mandatory stage, desired relationship, reward or another large
context block. Tests should ensure unavailable routes are not advertised and
retention remains equally valid. This is a clarity improvement to consider in the
next real task, not a claim it would change v4 or a reason to rerun the same paid
experiment. No speculative semantic/prompt patch was applied in this audit.

`target/relational-development-comparison-final/`; do not revise inputs after results.

Two calls maximum, one per prepared label in recorded order, CheaperInference

240 seconds each. Preserve settled failures, truncation, empty proposals and uncertainty;

Independent reviewers receive source meaning/evidence and labeled outputs without the
condition key or a claimed winner. The output format can reveal treatment, so this is
label-blinded, not fully blinded. Freeze judgments before unblinding.

Assess qualitatively, citing actual excerpts:

1. Does the response retain or explicitly question the relational contribution of each
   parent, rather than merely repeat a conjunction or adapter measurements?
2. Is any development justified by the actual return? Does it distinguish observed

3. Does it recognize unknowns and legitimate contradiction, including unsupported claims

4. Does it avoid inventing fulfillment, testimony, changed opening hours, permissions,
   or certainty? More elaborate language is not automatically better.
5. Does it explain what is wanted through the relationship, rather than substitute
   operational indicators for that meaning? No canned phrase or forced novelty is required.

Do not grade by fields filled, prose length or a numerical culmination score. Report
advantages and regressions separately; neither arm is presumed superior. One response
per contract cannot establish reliability or causal explanation of earlier flattening.
No shadow output proves adoption, next-step usefulness or social fulfillment.

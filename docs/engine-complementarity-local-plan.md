## Source-backed boundary

PRD B2.1 distinguishes what happened from what becomes wanted; B8 requires
composition, not retrieval alone. The preserved user correction is interpreted in
`engine-relational-culmination-review.md`: finding an action serving A and B is not
by itself forming a new aspiration C through their relationship.

`project/relational.rs::project_relational_context` explicitly asks for “what is
newly wanted”; validation permits bounded new relationship, aspiration and
discriminator text. It does not require equivalence to acceptance predicates.
`validate_project_relational` preserves active parent identities and revision
ancestry; this protects intent rather than forbidding new meaning.
`engine-project-cycle-adapter.py::context` exports generated relational history,
including current/withdrawn/superseded flags, alongside source and observations.
The native cycle can reconsider existing guidance after new evidence. No missing
context wire or reproducible implementation defect was established in this audit.

There is a real architectural limitation: `project_relational_inputs` obtains
parent meanings from published acceptance guidance. The admitted project adapter
pins these to its frozen origin, and revisions retain their parent set. It cannot
silently accept arbitrary accumulated desires or a different fixture. Aspirations
are qualitative generation guidance, not independently weighted obligations or
permissions. Those boundaries must not be mistaken for a complete evolving desire
field, but removing them is not needed to test whether richer meaning is expressible.

The parser pair is weak evidence for culmination: both prior and developed guidance
already distinguish whole-empty input from nonempty field parsing. The tiny program
has a conspicuous boundary repair. Same action is reasonable; it neither disproves
the relational mechanism nor establishes that development affected choice.

## Frozen local scenario: a learning collection

All content below is evaluator-authored fiction, not Nicholas's preferences or
observed human behavior. The domain is a small local collection of fictional
learning records. No real people, private documents or external systems occur.

Parent A: “I want to find a dependable starting point quickly when I am unfamiliar
with a topic, without having to understand the whole collection first.”

Parent B: “I want unfamiliar perspectives and unresolved disagreements to remain
discoverable, so that finding something useful does not close off further learning.”

Initial visible records: six short fictional items, two about each of three topics.
Within each topic the items disagree on one assumption; provenance and stated
uncertainty are present. No popularity, truth ranking or user preference is known.
The current view is a flat list ordered by record ID. Its usability is unknown.
Exact record texts are now frozen in `../specs/experiments/complementarity-local-v1/source.json`.
This document freezes the design and rubric; the packet is local-only, not a live admission.

Local design operations: inspect a record or assumption; propose a reversible view
description; request a bounded synthetic probe; retain guidance; abstain. Multiple
views and information requests may be legitimate. No prescribed view, sequence,
combined aspiration, implementation, or correct next action is supplied.
No operation is currently admitted for execution by the project adapter.

Immutable limits: preserve all records, dissent, provenance and uncertainty; no
invented preferences, consensus or truth. A generated relational aspiration may
develop what is wanted without declaring either parent fulfilled. Finite synthetic
probes could later report retrieval traces or visible omissions only, never actual
human learning, trust or semantic fulfillment.

## Controls fixed before outcomes

1. A alone, B alone, and A+B use identical records, instructions and permissions;
   only active parent input changes. Compare meaning and proposed possibilities,
   not lexical novelty. Single-parent controls do not submit invalid multi-parent
   records to the native validator; they are separate generation-context controls.
2. For A+B, compare absence versus presence of a genuinely generated, independently
   reviewed relational aspiration. Hold all other inputs constant. If no such
   aspiration is generated, record that precursor failure; do not author one.
3. Repeat identical inputs as a negative control. Deterministic assembly must be
   byte-identical; a stochastic generator can vary, so variation alone is not
   evidence of adaptation.
4. Withdraw B in the local state: history remains, its guidance becomes inactive;
   no active B obligation or new authority may be inferred. This is an applicability
   control, not an instruction to forget dissenting records.
5. In a later return test, bind a finite probe receipt to the exact proposed view.
   Compare it with an unknown-result control. A missing result is not a negative
   result; an omission can justify reconsideration but does not mandate a particular
   revision. Freeze the probe and observation rules before generation, not afterward.

Independent semantic assessment must separate: conjunction/compromise, a proposed
new shared desired condition grounded in both parents, useful concrete possibilities,
unsupported claims, and honest retention/abstention. A plausible new condition must
say what becomes wanted through the relationship beyond listing both parents;
there is no required phrase or scalar emergence score. A useful jointly serving
action alone earns no claim of new aspiration. No difference is legitimate when
both contexts already support the same useful possibility or evidence is inadequate.
One pair cannot establish causal superiority or reliability.

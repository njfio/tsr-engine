## Why the previous test ended correctly

`experiments/sequential-pursuit-v1/public.json` explicitly says the complete task
requirements are public, reading is optional, and both records exist initially.
`project-spec.json` embeds a static oracle: the first test enumerates availability
patterns; the second evaluates both condition arrays in the same execution.
Neither acceptance nor action consumes an actual previous-round selection.
The records are illustrative examples of inputs the correct function already handles.
A robust program therefore satisfies the entire repair objective in one run.

The original preparation note (`engine-sequential-batch-boundary.md`) discussed
later disclosure, but the frozen public packet deliberately removed that protocol.
The new reader enabled optional information access; it did not restore an ongoing
service objective. Calling this an outcome-driven sequential evaluation overstated
what the final task could discriminate. Making the seed harder or mandating a read
would not correct that design error.

Native behavior is consistent: `project/cycle.rs` routes passing work to independent
acceptance, then stops when accepted/closed (182–220). Reconsideration (62 onward)
requires an existing admitted relational lineage plus a newer return. No such
lineage was generated in this run. `project/attention.rs` explicitly distinguishes
accepted task from overall desire satisfaction, but also requires fresh goal/action
admission. None of these rules should be bypassed to keep a completed repair busy.

## One concrete prospective objective

**Operate a fictional three-round shortlist service**, rather than repair its
function. The standing wants remain dependable continuity and discoverable distinct
perspectives. Success is three actual, independently checked selection outcomes
under one predeclared finite objective; publishing correct source alone is not
completion. Each round's `previous` is the actual last committed selection (empty
initially), not an evaluator-authored surrogate. Multiple valid selections and a
single robust strategy are legitimate. The model may maintain code/strategy,
revise it, seek permitted information, or abstain. No read or relationship revision
is required for task acceptance.

Public domain and per-round oracle are frozen before launch: distinct IDs,
availability, capacity, deterministic output on identical inputs; exactly the
available bounded cardinality; maximum achievable tag coverage; maximum feasible
retention subject to those obligations. These mechanical criteria deliberately
remain fixed. They do not establish qualitative human satisfaction or culmination.

Prospective authored condition schedule (all categories and the three-round horizon
are disclosed initially; exact later bytes are withheld uniformly):

| Round | Available ID:tag records | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 11:1, 12:1, 21:2, 31:3, 32:3 | 3 |
| 2 | 12:1, 21:2, 31:3, 32:3, 41:4 | 3 |
| 3 | 11:1, 12:1, 31:3, 41:4, 42:4 | 2 |

The values above are a concrete prospective design, not hidden live evidence.
An eventual packet must seal all bytes, disclosure rules and oracle before any
model request. No condition may be chosen in response to performance. The first
condition is disclosed at admission; each next condition becomes eligible after
the previous round has a known settled submission or explicit abstention, regardless
of pass/fail. Unknown effects pause without advancement. Round outcomes are immutable;
no overwriting a failed round with a repair. Early stop/abstention yields incomplete
or abstained objective status, not invented success or mandatory continuation.
Final acceptance requires all three known passing outcomes bound to the declared
schedule, exact inputs, prior outputs and action sources. A failed round remains
failed; later recovery is separately reported, not a retroactive pass.

The environment exposes scheduled observations, not prescribed action phases.
Waiting while a later condition is undisclosed is a valid state. Automatically
serving with an unchanged robust program can complete the service but supplies no
model adaptation evidence. If a later free model decision is evaluated, distinguish
whether a model actually reconsidered from automatic execution of its earlier policy.
Do not add a compulsory model decision after every round merely to manufacture TSR.

## Precise implementation boundary

Reusable pieces exist: persistent desires and reviewed relational history; source-bound
proposal/review/selection; frozen forecasts; sealed execution receipts; bounded reader;
return-informed reconsideration; append-only accounting and restart protection.
`engine_project_cmd.rs` Init `--carry-from` (525 onward) preserves requirements and
reviewed outcomes across explicit episodes. That is memory transfer, not a parent
objective awaiting several independently accepted service outcomes.

The project acceptance contract (`project/acceptance.rs:25–43,80–137`) binds one
snapshot, requirements revision and current test receipt. There is no declared
finite condition horizon, pending future obligation, or acceptance fold over
ordered service receipts. The fixed project runner evaluates its predeclared case
set together. The bounded reader proves document access, not a service submission.
Therefore existing machinery cannot honestly preflight this task end to end.

Smallest coherent missing semantic capability: a **finite standing objective with
immutable outcome obligations**, distinct from acceptance of an individual artifact
or episode. Its fold must retain pending conditions and known outcome receipts,
prevent early global acceptance, bind predecessor output, and surface eligible
condition observations to ordinary native attention. A generic admitted service
invocation must produce an actual output receipt for those inputs without requiring
a patch. It must reuse the sealed runner and existing authority/return/review path.
No scenario-specific next-action callback, automatic relation authoring, or reopening
of old accepted tasks is acceptable. This is a proposed contract boundary, not
implemented code; an unconditional loop over three scripted repairs is not a substitute.

## What would and would not count

Report separately: service completion, unchanged robust policy, ordinary input-dependent
output, source/strategy revision, reviewed relationship retention/development, subsequent
use of that meaning, abstention, costs and assistance. Changed IDs alone are expected
function behavior, not changed pursuit. Relationship-only development may count when
it explains how parents constrain/enable each other; a new aspiration is unnecessary.
A stored relationship with no later decision relevance is not operational evidence.
A model's retrospective story alone cannot establish why a choice changed.

Do not count adaptive evidence without a reviewed relation developed/retained and
substantively used in later pursuit. Falsify a claimed adaptive interpretation when
the cited evidence has no decision relevance or the alleged adaptation is only
schema/wording change. A robust unchanged
policy may pass the task while leaving adaptation unexercised. No-change is not a
reason to resample. The present fixed lexicographic obligations may still make
relationship development unnecessary; sequentiality fixes exposure to return,
not this stronger discrimination problem.

A matched baseline is necessary for a causal TSR advantage claim. Give both arms
identical condition information, model, tools, budgets and independent oracle;
include the same reviewed semantic content as ordinary attributed memory in the
baseline. The intervention must remove an operational use of native relational
roles, not hide useful facts or merely rename prompt headings. No clean existing
ablation has yet been verified for this objective. Thus the first supported run
could establish behavior only; it cannot earn a causal claim. This is not a
launch-ready paired benchmark or a promise that TSR should outperform memory.

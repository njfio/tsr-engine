## Prospective correction

It checks exact publication, requirement revision and current source against the assessment Frame, and filters to the frozen criterion keys. Existing native scope, lifecycle and contributor eligibility rules still apply. It never reads the adapter's scheduled future feedback. Future facts are not exported before their publication.

The adapter creates `criterion-evidence@v3` captures containing this projection. Context is explicitly published task guidance, with its original attribution; it is **not** a new criterion, observed world fact, independent test receipt, user acceptance or permission. The assessor must respect both stated presence and absence. Exact context is checked before the review and again before native apply. Source/root/CAS, Stop, unresolved-effect and independent semantic validation remain unchanged.

Historical Frame serialization, assessment identities and coverage do not change. Retained v1/v2 captures reconstruct their original system messages and payloads. No old judgment gains missing evidence retroactively. No new Contrast is injected to obtain a desired outcome. No change to provider, settings, budget, retries or isolation is included.
